Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my session? +
We recommend booking at least 1-2 weeks in advance for portrait and birthday shoots, and 1-3 months ahead for weddings and major concert events to guarantee date availability.
How do I receive my final pictures? +
You will receive a private cloud gallery link where you can view, share with family, and download your high-resolution print-ready photos directly to your phone or computer.
Can you travel outside Ogun State? +
Yes! We regularly shoot in Lagos, Ibadan, and are available nationwide and internationally for destination weddings and concerts. Modest travel fees apply depending on distance.
What if I have never posed for photos before? +
No experience needed! Most of our clients have never done professional shoots before. Faruq provides continuous, easy-to-follow directions so your poses look completely natural and flattering.
