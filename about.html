The Creator's Journey

About Bank Shot It

Transforming brief moments into timeless masterpieces of light, emotion, and authenticity.

📸 Faruq Banky
Lead Visual Storyteller

"Photography is how we touch, feel, and love forever."

Hello, I'm Faruq — the creative founder and lead eye behind Bank Shot It (ShotByBankyCreativesConcept). Based in Ogun State and working across Nigeria, my journey began with an insatiable hunger to document people in their most genuine, unfiltered light.

To me, a photoshoot isn't about stiff poses or awkward countdowns. It's an intimate, fun collaboration where you are encouraged to laugh, move, breathe, and celebrate who you truly are. Whether it's the electric roar of a live concert, the quiet tears of a wedding vow, or the vibrant confidence of a milestone birthday — I capture the soul behind the smile.

When I'm not behind the camera, you'll find me exploring new landscapes, studying classic cinema lighting, and dreaming up fresh creative perspectives for our next shoot.

Our Core Principles

The Four Pillars Of My Craft

Every shoot is anchored on four unwavering commitments to artistic quality.

01

Natural & Ambient Lighting

Mastery over available sunlight and subtle off-camera lighting that flatters every skin tone and preserves cinematic realism.

02

Effortless Posing Direction

Never feel awkward again. I provide continuous, gentle guidance so your body language looks relaxed, confident, and editorial.

03

Meticulous Color Grading

Every frame is individually color balanced, enhancing rich skin tones, warm golden hues, and timeless contrast.

04

Private Cloud Delivery

Full-resolution, print-ready files delivered seamlessly to your phone or computer through a secure private gallery link.
